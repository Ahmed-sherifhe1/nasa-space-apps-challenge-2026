The Story

In June 2007, Tropical Cyclone Gonu struck Oman, becoming one of the most powerful tropical cyclones ever recorded in the Arabian Sea. The storm brought destructive winds, torrential rainfall, and coastal flooding to areas that were not accustomed to cyclones of such intensity.

Coastal infrastructure, roads, settlements, and essential services suffered extensive damage. The event demonstrated how vulnerable arid coastal regions can be to extreme weather.

Main Causes
- An exceptionally powerful tropical cyclone.
- Heavy rainfall and flash flooding.
- Coastal storm surge and powerful waves.
- Limited preparedness for a cyclone of this magnitude.
